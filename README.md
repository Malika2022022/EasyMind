# EasyMind 




A cross-platform mobile application designed to support individuals with dyslexia, making reading, writing, and daily academic life more accessible and engaging.

---

## About the Project

**EasyMind** was created for the **Technovation Girls** competition, where our team successfully reached the **quarterfinals**. I served as the **Main Full-Stack Developer** for the project. To bring this idea to life, I learned Flutter entirely from scratch in just one week to compete, proving my ability to adapt quickly and master new technologies under tight deadlines.

Many students struggle with reading, writing, and text comprehension due to dyslexia, which can impact their confidence and academic success. EasyMind is built to bridge this gap. The app allows users to download academic passages and practice core language skills—reading, listening, writing, and speaking—in a supportive, low-stress environment. It also integrates AI-powered assistance to offer interactive help and personalized guidance.

---

## Key Features

* **Accessibility-First UI/UX:** Tailored interfaces designed specifically to reduce cognitive load and improve readability for users with dyslexia.
* **Multi-Modal Skill Practice:** Dedicated modules for reading passages, audio playback, writing exercises, and speaking practice.
* **AI Integration:** Smart chat and assistance features to help users understand complex texts and provide interactive support.
* **Cloud & Authentication:** Powered by Firebase for secure user registration, authentication, and backend data management.
* **Cross-Platform Performance:** Built with Flutter and Dart, ensuring a smooth, native-like experience on both iOS and Android.

---

##  Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Flutter, Dart
* **Backend & Auth:** Firebase (Authentication, Cloud services)
* **Web View & Media:** flutter_inappwebview, audio players
* **Platform Support:** iOS & Android
